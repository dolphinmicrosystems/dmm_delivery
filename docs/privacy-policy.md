# Privacy Policy - Dunedin Milk & More app

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

This policy explains what the Dunedin Milk & More app ("the app") collects, why, and what
we do with it. The app is used by Dunedin Milk & More ("we", "us") and our delivery
drivers to plan and carry out milk deliveries in Dunedin, New Zealand. It is not a
public app: you can only sign in if we have invited you.

We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

## What we collect

**About drivers and staff who use the app**
- **Your Google account name and email address**, when you sign in with Google, so we know
  who you are and what you may see.
- **Your phone's precise location, only while you are driving a delivery run** with the app
  open. We use it to show the van on the map, give directions, confirm arrival at each stop,
  record the route driven, and let the owner see how the run is going. The app does not
  track your location when you are not on a run or when the app is closed.
- **Delivery photos** you take as proof of delivery. Each photo is stamped with the date,
  time and stop.
- **Delivery records**: when a run started and finished, when each stop was delivered,
  and where.
- **Whether the app is open**, so the owner can see which drivers are available.
- **A notification token** for your phone, so we can send you run and schedule
  notifications.
- **Crash reports and error logs** (through Google Firebase Crashlytics), which include
  your account ID but not your email address, so we can fix problems.

**About our customers**
- Run sheets contain customers' **names, delivery addresses, phone numbers, orders and
  delivery instructions**. Drivers see these so they can deliver the right order to the
  right place.

## How we use it

Only to run our deliveries: planning routes, giving drivers directions and orders,
keeping proof of delivery, letting the owner follow runs, sending notifications, and
fixing problems with the app. We do not sell any information, we do not use it for
advertising, and the app has no ads.

## Who we share it with

- **Google**: the app runs on Google Cloud and Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage,
  notifications, crash reports) and uses Google Maps (maps, addresses and routes). Google
  processes this information for us under its own terms. It is stored on Google Cloud
  servers in the United States.
- **Our email provider**, only to send driver invitation emails.
- Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep delivery records and photos while we need them for our business records, and
then delete them. If you stop driving for us, we turn off your access. You can ask us to
delete your personal information at any time, unless we need to keep it by law.

## Security

Information travels encrypted between the app and Google's servers. Access is limited
by sign-in: drivers only see the runs they are given, and only the owner sees the whole
business.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct
or delete it. Contact us below. If you are not happy with our response, you can
complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).
